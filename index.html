01 / THE PRODUCT

Beyond a swipe.

Devigital identifies producer and writer Oren Peli and his team as the client. The product envisioned a matchmaking experience for adults built around conversations. Prospects changed as members adjusted preferences such as age, race and language. Mutual matches could schedule video chats or meet during recurring Mixer events, with the option to leave a conversation and continue meeting other prospects.

The team built the cross-platform app with React Native, deployed cloud functions, integrated a third-party tool for advanced prospect filtering, and implemented a waitlist or invitation flow. Devigital identifies timely delivery and stable simultaneous features as key project results.

02 / QUALITY CHALLENGE

Every path.
Every person.

The product had to keep Mixer sessions and video calling stable while matching and search responded correctly to changing preferences. The delivery deadline made dependable regression and clear defect communication essential.

As the sole QA engineer on Spot.Love, I created the testing artifacts, checked functional and edge-case journeys across Android, iOS and web administration, and validated fixes through stage, pre-production and production. Later, I introduced mobile automation with Katalon Studio and Appium.

  • Mixer participation and multi-user calling
  • Scheduled and randomly paired video conversations
  • Age, race and language preferences, search and matching
  • Profiles, messaging, administration and release regression
03 / OUTCOME

Delivered
with confidence.

Devigital reports that the team delivered the app on time, stabilized core features and completed rigorous QA before release. Its published case study also lists 45% increased sales, 34% traffic in the 18–24 demographic and 52%+ increased retention. These are company-reported project figures, not individual QA results.
